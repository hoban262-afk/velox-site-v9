Why pay by bank?

Open banking lets you pay directly from your current account without entering any card details. Your bank authenticates the payment — the same way you'd approve any transaction in your banking app.

It's regulated by the FCA (the same body that regulates UK banks), so it carries the same protections as a standard bank transfer — with the speed and simplicity of a one-click payment.

Money goes directly to us. No card processors, no stored card data, no risk of card fraud.
