How it works

Three steps.
One brand-new peptide.

The whole thing happens in one sitting. You don't need a science degree to use it — if you can describe what you're after, you can use it.

01 · TELL IT WHAT YOU WANT
🎯

Describe it in normal words

Type what you're trying to study in everyday English — what you want the peptide to do, or the name of one you already know. No special codes or science-speak needed. The AI works out the rest.

02 · THE AI INVENTS DESIGNS
🧬

It dreams up three new peptides

In one go you get three new peptide "recipes." They're built to be different from each other on purpose — different shapes, sizes and behaviours — so you get a real spread of ideas, not three copies of the same one.

03 · IT GRADES AND CHECKS THEM
📊

See the best — and what to order

Each design is graded on how easy it is to make, how well it should dissolve and how sturdy it is, and checked against peptides that already exist so you know which are truly new. Then we name the closest compound you can order today — HPLC-verified and shipped from the UK — to benchmark your design against.
