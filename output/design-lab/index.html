How it works

Three stages.
One novel compound.

The entire design-to-synthesis pipeline runs in a single session. No computational-biology degree required.

01 · TARGET INTERPRETATION
🎯

Describe your target

Write what you want in plain English — mechanism, desired properties, reference compounds, constraints. The AI converts it into a structured design brief.

02 · SEQUENCE GENERATION
🧬

AI designs the sequences

Six structurally diverse novel candidates are generated across different scaffold classes — with hard diversity enforcement so you never get six variants of the same compound.

Every candidate is scored across synthesisability, solubility, GRAVY, net charge and protease risk — then checked for novelty against known research peptides. Export and order.
