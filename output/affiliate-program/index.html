Frequently asked

How much do I earn?

You earn a commission on the subtotal of every order placed with your code. Your exact rate is agreed when your application is approved, and it's always visible in your dashboard.

When and how am I paid?

Commission is confirmed once an order is dispatched. Payouts are then sent manually by our team to your nominated PayPal email or bank reference. Your dashboard shows pending, confirmed and paid amounts at all times.

Does it cost anything to join?

No — the program is completely free, with no minimum sales and no lock-in.

What if an order is cancelled or refunded?

Commission on a cancelled or refunded order is automatically reversed and not paid. It's shown transparently in your dashboard so there are never any surprises.

How do I share my code?

However suits your audience — a post, story, bio link, video description or newsletter. Anyone who enters your code at checkout is tracked to you automatically.
