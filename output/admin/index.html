A big featured block on the homepage. Pick a product and a discount %, optionally a headline and a countdown. Display-only promo - it advertises the deal but doesn’t change the checkout price (set the real Sale price in the Pricing tab if you want it charged).

Product pricing

One source of truth for every price on the site. Base = the normal selling price. Sale = an active deal (leave blank for none). RRP = an optional higher "was" for a strikethrough. Untick Disc. to exclude an item from all deal/volume/code discounts (e.g. the pen). Changes go live across the site on the next page load.
