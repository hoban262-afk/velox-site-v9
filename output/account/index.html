How your points work

  • Earn 1 point for every £1 you spend (on the order subtotal, before discounts).
  • Tier bonus — the higher your tier, the more you earn per £: Silver 1.25×, Gold 1.5×, Platinum 2×.
  • Redeem at checkout — 100 points = £1 off, from a minimum of 500 points.
  • Points are added once your order is paid. Your tier is based on the points you’ve earned over time (not your current balance), so it never goes down when you spend points.
