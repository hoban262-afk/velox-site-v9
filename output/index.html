First order? Here’s what to know

Common questions from researchers ordering for the first time.

Is this legal?
Yes. Research compounds are legal to purchase in the UK for in vitro research purposes. All products are sold strictly as research reagents for use by qualified researchers in laboratory settings. They are not medicinal products under the Human Medicines Regulations 2012. See our Research Use Policy for full details.
How does payment work?
We accept Pay by Bank — an instant, secure open-banking payment processed via Fena. You authorise the payment in your own banking app; no card details are stored on our site. Manual UK bank transfer to our Zempler business account is also available as a fallback. Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of cleared payment.
When will it arrive?
Orders are dispatched within 24 hours — same day if placed before 2 pm on a working day. Delivery is via Royal Mail Tracked 24, typically 1–2 working days from dispatch. You receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order is shipped.
What’s included with my order?
Every order includes a batch-specific Certificate of Analysis documenting HPLC purity, mass spectrometry confirmation, and appearance results. The CoA is emailed to you with your order confirmation and is also available in our CoA library.
