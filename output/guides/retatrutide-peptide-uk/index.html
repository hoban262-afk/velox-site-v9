Retatrutide is a synthetic peptide engineered to simultaneously activate three hormone receptors: the glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor (GLP-1R), the glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide receptor (GIPR), and the glucagon receptor (GcgR). This triple mechanism distinguishes it from approved GLP-1 agents such as semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy) and the dual GLP-1/GIP agonist tirzepatide (Mounjaro).

One molecule, three receptor targets

In Phase 2 research published in the New England Journal of Medicine (2023), participants receiving retatrutide 12 mg weekly demonstrated a mean weight reduction of approximately 22.1% over 48 weeks - a result that attracted significant scientific attention and drove strong search interest from UK researchers.

UK Regulatory Status

Understanding the regulatory position of retatrutide in the UK is critical for any researcher intending to acquire or work with the compound.

MHRA Position

Retatrutide is not licensed as a medicinal product by the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA). It is in Phase 3 clinical development under Eli Lilly's oversight. No marketing authorisation has been granted. It cannot legally be administered to humans in the UK outside of an authorised clinical trial.

Is It Legal to Buy Retatrutide in the UK?

Yes, under the correct conditions. Research chemicals - including peptides such as retatrutide - may be purchased and possessed in the UK for genuine in vitro laboratory research purposes. The compound must not be:

Storage and Handling

Lyophilised Powder (Unopened)

Store at −20 °C in the dark. Properly stored lyophilised retatrutide is stable for up to 24 months. Do not expose to repeated temperature fluctuations.

Reconstituted Solution

Reconstitute using sterile bacteriostatic water (0.9% benzyl alcohol). Working aliquots in bacteriostatic water can be stored at 4 °C for up to 4 weeks. For longer storage, prepare small aliquots and store at −20 °C; avoid more than two freeze-thaw cycles.

Published Research Overview

Retatrutide has a substantial published research record across preclinical and clinical stages:

Cell Metabolism · 2022

Preclinical: Triple Agonism in Obese Rodent Models

Coskun et al. demonstrated that retatrutide produced superior weight loss and metabolic improvements over GLP-1 and dual GLP-1/GIP agonists in diet-induced obese mice and non-human primates, establishing the preclinical rationale for human trials.

PMID: 35108511
The Lancet · 2022

Phase 1: Safety and Pharmacokinetics

Urva et al. published the Phase 1 results for LY3437943, confirming a weekly dosing pharmacokinetic profile, dose-proportional exposure, and an acceptable safety profile in healthy volunteers and participants with type 2 diabetes.

PMID: 36356631
New England Journal of Medicine · 2023

Phase 2: Weight Reduction Outcomes

Jastreboff et al. reported 48-week results from the Phase 2 NEJM trial. The 12 mg retatrutide cohort achieved a mean 22.1% reduction in body weight, with dose-dependent efficacy across all treatment arms. Phase 3 recruitment is ongoing.

PMID: 37366315

Frequently Asked Questions

Is retatrutide legal in the UK?

Retatrutide is not a licensed medicine in the UK and has not received MHRA approval. It is legal to purchase and possess as a research chemical for in vitro laboratory use. It may not be administered to humans outside of an authorised clinical trial.

What purity should research-grade retatrutide be?

Research-grade retatrutide should be verified at ≥99% purity by HPLC. Each batch should have a Certificate of Analysis confirming purity, sequence identity, and mass spectrometry validation.

What is retatrutide used for in research?

In research settings, retatrutide is studied as a triple receptor agonist at GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors. Published preclinical and clinical data have examined its effects on body weight, glucose metabolism, and lipid profiles.

Is retatrutide available in the UK as a medicine?

No. As of 2026, retatrutide (LY3437943) is in Phase 3 clinical trials and has not received marketing authorisation from the MHRA. It is not available as a licensed medicine in the UK.

How should retatrutide be stored?

Lyophilised retatrutide powder should be stored at −20 °C in the dark. Once reconstituted in bacteriostatic water, working aliquots should be kept at 4 °C and used within 4 weeks. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.

What is the molecular weight of retatrutide?

Retatrutide (LY3437943) has a molecular weight of approximately 4,813 Da. It is a 36-amino-acid peptide with fatty-acid conjugation enabling simultaneous GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptor agonism.

How does retatrutide differ from tirzepatide and semaglutide?

Semaglutide is a selective GLP-1 receptor agonist. Tirzepatide is a dual GLP-1/GIP agonist. Retatrutide adds glucagon receptor agonism, creating a triple agonist. Phase 2 data showed greater mean weight reduction than either comparator.
