About Velox Peptides

Velox Peptides is a United Kingdom research reagent supplier operating as CRP Labs Ltd (Company No. NI738125). We supply HPLC-verified research peptides to laboratory and qualified research customers across the UK, dispatched from Northern Ireland by Royal Mail Tracked 24.

Declan Hoban

Founder

Honestly, I started Velox because I’d had enough of how the research-peptide world tends to work. So much of what’s out there is sold on trust you can’t actually check, by people who disappear the second something goes wrong. I wanted to do the opposite, and build something real with my name on it.

It began with just me. As more researchers came on board and started trusting us, I brought in people who genuinely care about this community, not just the next sale. We’re all on the same page about what matters. I’d far rather earn someone’s trust once and keep it for years than chase a quick order. The consistency, the honesty and actually being there when you need us mean just as much to us as what’s in the vial.

And honestly, Velox was never really about selling vials. That’s the easy part. What I set out to build was the thing this space has always been missing: proper tools, like our Design Lab where you can design and pressure-test a novel peptide in plain English, guides that explain the science without the hype, and a community where researchers actually talk to each other instead of guessing in the dark. The Discord, the inner circle on Telegram, the people we bring in along the way, that’s the part I care about most. We supply research-grade peptides to keep the whole thing running, but the tools and the community are the point. Everything else is just how we fund it.

So if you’ve got a question before you order, whether it’s about a batch, a compound, or what you’re working on, just email support@veloxpeps.com. You’ll get a real, thought-through reply from someone who actually cares about getting it right for you.

Who we are

Velox is a real, registered company with real people behind it. We supply research-grade peptides to UK and EU researchers, and we hold ourselves to the standard a working lab expects from any reagent supplier. Every batch we send out is HPLC tested by Janoshik Analytical before it leaves us, and we publish the purity figures so you can check what you’re getting instead of taking our word for it.

We’re registered in Northern Ireland as CRP Labs Ltd (Company No. NI738125), and you can verify that yourself at Companies House. If you’ve got a question before you order, you’ll hear back from an actual person, not a bot. Just email support@veloxpeps.com.

Why we built Velox

The research-peptide market has a trust problem, and anyone who’s been around it long enough knows it. So much of what’s sold online is underdosed, mislabelled, or never independently tested at all, and the people selling it tend to vanish the moment something goes wrong. We built Velox to be the opposite of that: a real, registered company that publishes an independent purity figure for every batch and puts its name behind the result.

Our rule is simple. We won’t send out a batch we wouldn’t be happy to stand behind ourselves. Every lot is HPLC tested by an independent lab (Janoshik Analytical) before we list it, the certificate of analysis is there for any customer who asks, and we keep the historical records in our CoA library so nothing has to be taken on faith. When something’s out of stock, we’ll tell you straight. We’d rather lose the sale than ship something that doesn’t meet the bar.

What we supply

Research-grade peptide compounds for in vitro scientific research, along with the reconstitution supplies (bacteriostatic water) you need in the lab. Everything we sell is a research reagent and nothing more. None of it is a medicinal product within the meaning of the Human Medicines Regulations 2012.

How we test

Every batch is HPLC tested by an independent lab before it’s dispatched. The certificate of analysis covers HPLC purity, mass spectrometry confirmation of molecular weight, and appearance. If you want the paperwork for your batch, just ask, and you’ll find the historical records archived in our CoA library.

How we dispatch

We dispatch orders within 24 hours of payment clearing, Monday to Friday. UK delivery goes out via Royal Mail Tracked 24, usually 1-2 working days. The full breakdown is in our shipping information.

How to order

We accept Pay by Bank, an instant, secure open-banking payment handled through Fena and authorised right in your own banking app, with no card details needed. If you’d rather, manual UK bank transfer to our Zempler business account (CRP Labs Ltd) works too, and EU customers can use the IBAN/BIC details shown at checkout. Either way, your order goes out within 24 hours of cleared payment and a tracking number lands in your inbox.

Contact

Got a question before you buy, need a batch CoA, or want to talk through a research protocol? Email support@veloxpeps.com and a real person will get back to you.
