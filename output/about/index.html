About Velox Peptides

Velox Peptides is a United Kingdom research reagent supplier operating as CRP Labs Ltd (Company No. NI738125). We supply HPLC-verified research peptides to laboratory and qualified research customers across the UK, dispatched from Northern Ireland by Royal Mail Tracked 24.

Who we are

Velox Peptides was founded with a straightforward goal: supply research-grade peptides to UK and EU researchers with the same rigour a laboratory expects from any reagent supplier. Every batch we dispatch is third-party HPLC tested by Janoshik Analytical before it leaves. We publish the purity figures because we believe researchers should be able to verify what they receive — not take a supplier’s word for it.

We are a small, UK-based operation registered in Northern Ireland (CRP Labs Ltd, Company No. NI738125). That registration is verifiable at Companies House. If you have a question before ordering, you will get a reply from a person, not an automated system. Email veloxpeps@gmail.com.

What we supply

Research-grade peptide compounds for in vitro scientific research, together with reconstitution supplies (bacteriostatic water) required for laboratory use. Every compound is sold strictly as a research reagent and is not a medicinal product within the meaning of the Human Medicines Regulations 2012.

How we test

Every batch is third-party HPLC-tested prior to dispatch. Certificates of analysis document HPLC purity, mass spectrometry confirmation of molecular weight, and appearance. Every batch is third-party HPLC-verified. Batch documentation is available on request; historical batch records are archived in our CoA library.

How we dispatch

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of payment confirmation, Monday to Friday. Standard UK delivery is via Royal Mail Tracked 24 — 1-2 working days. Full details are in our shipping information.

How to order

We accept Pay by Bank — an instant, secure open-banking payment processed via Fena, authorised in your own banking app with no card details required. Manual UK bank transfer to our Zempler business account (CRP Labs Ltd) is also available as a fallback; EU customers can use the IBAN/BIC details provided at checkout. Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of cleared payment, with a tracking number sent by email.

Contact

For pre-purchase enquiries, batch CoA requests, or research protocol queries, contact veloxpeps@gmail.com.
