Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about Velox Peptides research compounds, testing, delivery, and payment.

Ordering & payment

How do I place an order?
Add the research compounds you need to your order, then proceed to checkout and enter your delivery details. You confirm your eligibility as a qualified researcher and complete payment by bank. Once payment clears, your order is dispatched within 24 hours, Monday to Friday.
How do I pay for research peptides?
Payment is by Pay by Bank — instant, secure open-banking authorised in your own banking app (no card details required). Manual UK bank transfer is also available as a fallback; EU customers can use the IBAN/BIC details shown at checkout. Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of cleared payment.
Do you take card payments or cryptocurrency?
No. We do not process card payments or cryptocurrency. All orders are paid by Pay by Bank (open banking authorised in your banking app) or by manual bank transfer. This keeps checkout simple and secure, with no card details to enter.
Will I get an order confirmation?
Yes. You receive an order confirmation by email once your order is placed, and a further update once payment has cleared and your order is dispatched. Tracking details are included with your dispatch confirmation.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Orders that have not yet been dispatched can be changed or cancelled by emailing veloxpeps@gmail.com as soon as possible. Once an order has dispatched, research compounds are non-returnable for handling and chain-of-custody reasons. Full details are in our refunds policy.
Can I order on behalf of a company or laboratory?
Yes. If you require an invoice made out to a company or research institution, include the company name and billing details at checkout, or email veloxpeps@gmail.com after ordering.

Shipping & delivery

Where do you ship to?
We ship to UK addresses (including the Channel Islands and Isle of Man) and to EU member states and EEA countries (Norway, Iceland, Switzerland). We do not ship outside the UK and EU/EEA. Full details are on our shipping page.
How quickly will my order be dispatched?
Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of payment confirmation, Monday to Friday. UK delivery is sent via Royal Mail Tracked 24. EU orders ship via Royal Mail International Tracked.
Do you offer free UK shipping?
Yes. UK shipping is free on orders over £80. Orders below that threshold are charged a flat tracked-shipping rate shown at checkout. All UK orders are sent via Royal Mail Tracked 24.
Do you ship internationally to the EU?
Yes. We ship to EU member states and EEA countries via Royal Mail International Tracked. EU customers are responsible for compliance with their local import regulations. Pricing and IBAN/BIC payment details are shown at checkout.
Is packaging discreet?
Yes. All orders are sent in plain, discreet packaging with no product details visible on the outside. The contents are not described on the exterior of the parcel.
Can I track my order?
Yes. Every order is sent on a tracked service — Royal Mail Tracked 24 for the UK and Royal Mail International Tracked for the EU. A tracking number is included with your dispatch confirmation email.

Product quality & testing

What purity standard are Velox Peptides compounds?
All compounds are HPLC-tested to a minimum purity of ≥99%. The exact batch-verified purity is shown on each compound page and available on request.
Who carries out your testing?
Testing is carried out by independent third-party laboratory Janoshik Analytical. This keeps verification separate from the supplier so results are impartial. Learn more about the process in our guide to how peptides are tested.
What do HPLC and mass spectrometry confirm?
HPLC (high-performance liquid chromatography) measures the purity of a compound by separating and quantifying its components. Mass spectrometry confirms the molecular identity and weight. Together they verify that a batch matches its stated identity and purity.
Is a Certificate of Analysis available?
Yes. A Certificate of Analysis (CoA) and batch documentation are available on request. Previous batch records are archived in our CoA library.
Is batch documentation provided for each compound?
Yes. Every batch is third-party HPLC-verified and has its own documentation. The batch-verified purity is shown on the relevant compound page, and the underlying documentation is available on request and archived in the CoA library.

Storage & handling

How should I store lyophilised peptides?
Lyophilised (freeze-dried) peptides should be kept desiccated and protected from light, and stored cold. See our peptide storage and handling guide for full details, and the individual compound page for compound-specific notes.
How should I store reconstituted peptides?
Once reconstituted with bacteriostatic water, solutions should be kept refrigerated and protected from light. Storage times vary by compound, so always check the relevant compound page and our storage and handling guide.
What is bacteriostatic water and what is it for?
Bacteriostatic water is sterile water containing a small amount of preservative, used in the laboratory to reconstitute lyophilised compounds. For more detail, see our guide on bacteriostatic water explained.
How do I reconstitute a peptide?
Reconstitution involves adding a measured volume of bacteriostatic water to the lyophilised compound. To work out the volume for a target concentration, use our reconstitution calculator. Follow standard laboratory handling practice throughout.
What is the shelf life of your compounds?
Shelf life depends on the compound and on storage conditions. Lyophilised compounds kept correctly are generally more stable than reconstituted solutions. Refer to the individual compound page and our storage and handling guide for compound-specific guidance.
