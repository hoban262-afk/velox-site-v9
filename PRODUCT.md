# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Qualified researchers and lab buyers comparing peptide suppliers. They arrive
sceptical — the category is full of grey-market sellers with stock photos and
neon "lab" aesthetics — and they are weighing three things in this order:
1. Is the product real? (purity, identity, independent testing, documentation)
2. Is it good value? (price per mg, volume tiers, shipping)
3. Can I trust these people? (clear company, honest voice, real support)

Context: mostly desktop research sessions, cross-referencing CoAs and prices
across tabs. A meaningful and growing share are international (60+ countries),
all priced in GBP.

## Product Purpose

Velox Peptides (CRP Labs Ltd, NI738125) sells research peptides for in-vitro
use, dispatched from the UK worldwide. Every batch is independently tested by
Janoshik Analytical (HPLC purity + mass-spec identity) and the results are
viewable in a public CoA Library. The site's job is to convert sceptical,
informed buyers by *showing* rigour — real test data, exact pricing, plain
honest copy — rather than asserting it with badges.

Success: the site feels like the most credible, least hype-driven supplier in
the category. A first-time researcher should think "these people clearly know
what they're doing and aren't hiding anything."

## Brand Personality

Quietly expensive. Exacting. Honest to a fault. Three words: **precise,
confident, candid.** The voice is the founder (Declan) on a message — British,
plain, zero hype, would rather lose the sale than oversell. Premium apothecary
meets analytical lab: the calm authority of a brand that doesn't need to shout
because the data does the talking.

Emotional goal: reassurance through competence. The visitor should relax —
"I can trust this" — not get hyped.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI / crypto-SaaS dark theme**: neon glow-on-black, cyan particles,
  purple→blue gradients, glassmorphism, glowing box-shadows. This is the
  current site's biggest liability and the category's biggest cliché.
- **Grey-market peptide sellers**: stock-photo vials, fake urgency, "90% OFF"
  starbursts, neon "LABORATORY GRADE" stamps, countdown timers.
- **Hype supplement / nootropic marketing**: bro-science energy, before/after
  hype, manufactured scarcity, five exclamation marks.
- **Over-rounded friendly startup**: pill-everything, bouncy easing, cartoon
  illustration, pastel gradients. Velox is precise, not playful.

## Design Principles

1. **The data is the hero.** Show real CoAs, real purity figures, real prices.
   Evidence over adjectives. If a claim can be proven on screen, prove it.
2. **Quiet confidence.** Restraint reads as premium. One accent, used sparingly,
   beats colour everywhere. Lighting is purposeful, never decorative glow.
3. **Honest by construction.** Never manufacture urgency, scarcity, ratings or
   testimonials. The honesty *is* the marketing (UK law and brand both demand it).
4. **Earn trust in the first screen.** Company, testing partner, dispatch and
   price clarity should be legible before any scroll.
5. **Precision in the craft.** Tight typography, deliberate spacing, exact
   alignment. The interface should feel as rigorous as the QC it describes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1, including muted/caption text
  and placeholders — the current low-contrast grey-on-dark is a known failure.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation.
- Keyboard-navigable commerce flow; visible focus states.
- Never rely on colour alone to convey state (stock, sale, errors).
