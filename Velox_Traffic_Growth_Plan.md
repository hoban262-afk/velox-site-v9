# Velox Peptides — Traffic & Growth Plan

*Built from live data (first-party tracking + orders), June 2026.*

## The one-line diagnosis
Your **site converts fine** for the traffic it gets (6 real orders, ~£905, £151 AOV in ~2 weeks). The bottleneck is **not the funnel — it's the top of it**. You had only **~88 real external visitors in 5 days**. Everything below is about getting more *qualified* people in the door, cheaply, given peptides can't use normal Meta/Google paid ads.

### What the data says
- **Best external source: X/Twitter (19 visitors).** Google sent only **6** — the site is new and not ranking yet.
- **Retatrutide is ~50% of revenue.** GHK-Cu, BPC-157, TB-500 are the supporting cast. Bac water attaches well.
- **Payment drop-off:** the few cancellations are customers not finishing the Fena (open-banking) approval in their bank app — normal, but reducible.
- **Email list is tiny (16).** Reviews tiny (4, all 5★). Subscribe-&-Save just got fixed → recurring revenue is now unlocked.

## Channel strategy (in priority order)

### 1. SEO — the compounding long game (biggest ceiling)
You already have ~90 indexed-worthy pages and a strong guide library. Make Google actually send traffic:
- **Google Search Console:** verify the property, submit `sitemap.xml`, and check Coverage — confirm all product + guide URLs are *indexed*, not just crawled. Fix anything excluded.
- **Win the money keywords** (high buyer intent): `buy retatrutide uk`, `tirzepatide uk`, `bpc-157 uk`, `retatrutide vs ozempic/wegovy/tirzepatide`. You already have the comparison guides — make sure their `<title>`/meta target these exactly, and that each guide links to the matching product page.
- **Double down on comparison/educational content** — it ranks, pulls buyers researching, and you already do it well. Add 1–2 new comparison/"is X legal/safe in the UK" pieces per month around your hero compounds.
- **Internal linking:** every guide should funnel to a product (and vice-versa). This spreads ranking authority to the pages that make money.
- **Backlinks (slow but durable):** relevant Reddit/forum answers, niche directories, a couple of guest mentions. Even 5–10 quality links moves a new site.

### 2. X / Twitter — your proven channel, lean in hard
It's already out-pulling Google 3:1. Treat it as the #1 acquisition lever *right now*.
- **Cadence:** 1–2 posts/day, every day. Consistency beats perfection.
- **Content pillars (rotate):**
  1. *Education* — short, useful peptide facts (no health claims; "research use" framing).
  2. *Proof/trust* — CoA screenshots, ≥99% HPLC, "dispatched from NI, tracked 24."
  3. *Range* — spotlight a compound (lead with Retatrutide), link to the PDP.
  4. *Novelty* — the Design Lab is genuinely unique; post designs/teasers — it's shareable.
- **Profile:** bio link → shop; **pin a Retatrutide + CoA post**.
- **Engage**, don't just broadcast — reply to peptide/biohacking accounts daily to get in front of their audiences.

### 3. Email & retention — cheap revenue you're underusing
- **Grow the list:** you built a free handbook lead magnet — actually *promote* it (X, site banner, post-purchase). Every visitor who won't buy today should leave their email.
- **Send the monthly newsletter** (template exists) — keeps you top-of-mind for re-orders.
- **Push Subscribe-&-Save** now that it works: it's recurring revenue and increases LTV. Add a line to product pages + a post-purchase email.
- **Ask for reviews** (flow exists) — 4 reviews is too few for trust. Every dispatched order should get the request; aim for 25+.

### 4. Affiliates / referrals — borrow other people's audiences
You have an affiliate program built. Since paid ads are off the table, **affiliates and micro-influencers in the fitness/biohacking niche are the scalable acquisition channel.**
- Recruit 5–10 affiliates with relevant followings (you have the recruiting kit).
- Give them the CoA/quality angle — it's your differentiator.

## Conversion tune-ups (small, high-leverage)
- **Cut Fena drop-off:** at the pay step, add explicit copy — "**Approve the payment in your banking app to finish — it takes ~20 seconds.**" Most "cancellations" are people who didn't realise they had to confirm in-app.
- **Don't cancel unpaid orders early** (see recovery note below) — let the 3-email sequence run first.
- **Social proof above the fold** on PDPs: review count + "5,310+ orders shipped" + CoA badge.
